# Operator guide — Espresso Throwdown

## Organiser

1. Create a free member profile.
2. Run setup: host, coffee, shared equipment, format.
3. For Premium Tournaments, pay USD 300. Wait until the desk shows **paid**. The return URL is not enough.
4. Invite competitors, one Cup Steward, and the judge pool.
5. Lock the roster, then generate the bracket.
6. For each heat: assign eligible judges, stage (this creates new cup codes), wait for the Cup Steward, start, mark brewing complete, open judging, reveal the calculated result.
7. Both recipes must be locked before the next heat can start.
8. Complete the event only when every heat and recipe is done. Recipes publish together.

You cannot enter a judge's ballot. If something goes wrong, void the heat with a reason. That starts a new attempt with new codes.

## Cup Steward

You are the only ordinary user who sees which person belongs to which cup code.

1. Open the Cup Steward screen for the event.
2. Write the codes on the cups exactly as shown.
3. Confirm when service is coded.
4. Do not photograph the mapping, read it aloud to the room, or leave the screen visible to judges.

You cannot compete or judge in the same event.

## Judges

You will see two random codes and, for Official WEC Scoring v3, three categories. Choose one cup in each category. Submit once. The ballot cannot be changed.

## Competitors

After brewing is marked complete, submit your recipe from your own account. It locks. The organiser can request a correction; you must confirm it.

## Public board

Share `/throwdown/e/{slug}`. It shows roster, live heat, ballot counts, and results. It never shows live choices, mappings, or recipes before the event is complete.
